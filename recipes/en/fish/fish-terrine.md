# Fish terrine

A rich fish terrine with cream, broccoli, speck, and herbs.

## Ingredients

- 1½–2 kg fish (e.g. hake; half ling, half cod)
- 3 eggs
- 4 dl cream
- Salt and pepper
- 250 g broccoli
- 1 lemon
- 400 g speck, in flakes
- 2 tbsp chervil
- 1 sprig thyme
- 1 sprig marjoram
- ½ coriander (as noted)
- 1 dl white wine
