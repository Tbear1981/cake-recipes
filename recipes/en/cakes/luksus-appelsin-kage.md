# Luksus appelsin cake

A rich orange cake with chocolate and vanilla.

## Ingredients

- 250 g butter or margarine
- 200 g icing sugar
- 4 eggs
- Juice of 1 orange
- 100 g chopped chocolate
- 1 vanilla pod
- 125 g wheat flour
- 125 g potato starch
- 1 tsp baking powder

## Method

1. Cream butter and icing sugar; beat in eggs one at a time.
2. Add orange juice, chocolate, and vanilla.
3. Fold in flours and baking powder.
4. Bake in a greased springform about 1 hour at about 190°C.
5. Dust with icing sugar when cold.
