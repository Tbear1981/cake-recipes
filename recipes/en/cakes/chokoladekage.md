# Chocolate cake

*Ugens lille lækkeri* — a moist cocoa loaf cake; freezes well.

## Ingredients

- 150 g butter or margarine
- 175 g sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla sugar
- 2 eggs
- 175 g flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 4 tbsp cocoa
- ¾ dl milk
- ¾ dl boiling water

## Method

1. Cream butter, sugar, and vanilla sugar; beat in eggs.
2. Stir in flour, baking powder, and cocoa alternately with milk and boiling water.
3. Bake in a loaf tin 1–1¼ hours at 175°C.
4. Suitable for freezing once cool (per the original note).
