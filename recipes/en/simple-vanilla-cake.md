# Simple Vanilla Cake

A light, everyday sponge cake with vanilla.

## Ingredients

- 200 g all-purpose flour
- 200 g granulated sugar
- 200 g unsalted butter, softened
- 4 large eggs
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 2–3 tbsp milk (if needed)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease and line a 20 cm round tin.
2. Beat butter and sugar until pale and fluffy. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then vanilla.
3. Sift flour, baking powder, and salt together. Fold into the butter mixture. Add milk only if the batter is stiff.
4. Spread into the tin and bake 25–30 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.
5. Cool in the tin 10 minutes, then turn out onto a rack. Serve plain or with icing.
