# Alexandra pudding

A delicate set pudding with fruit wine, vanilla, and lemon zest. Serve cold with cherry sauce.

## Ingredients

- 2 eggs, separated
- 125 g sugar
- Vanilla (to taste)
- 500 ml whole milk (“sweet milk”)
- 100 ml fruit wine
- A little grated lemon zest
- 8 gelatin sheets

## Method

1. Soak the gelatin sheets in cold water until soft. Squeeze out excess water.
2. Beat the egg yolks with the sugar until pale and thick.
3. Warm the milk with the fruit wine, vanilla, and lemon zest (do not boil). Dissolve the softened gelatin in the warm mixture.
4. Gradually stir the warm mixture into the yolk and sugar mixture.
5. Whip the egg whites to stiff peaks. Fold gently into the custard.
6. Pour into a bowl and chill until set.
7. Serve cold with cherry sauce (*kirsebærsauce*).
