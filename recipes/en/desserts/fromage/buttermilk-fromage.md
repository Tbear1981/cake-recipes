# Buttermilk fromage

A light, tangy fromage made with buttermilk and cream, set with gelatin and served with macaroons.

## Ingredients

- 500 ml buttermilk
- 100 ml cream
- 4 gelatin sheets
- Macaroons (for serving and lining, as desired)
- Sugar, to taste

## Method

1. Soak the gelatin sheets in cold water until soft. Squeeze out excess water.
2. Warm a little of the cream (not boiling) and dissolve the softened gelatin in it.
3. Mix buttermilk and the remaining cream. Sweeten with sugar to taste.
4. Stir in the dissolved gelatin until evenly combined.
5. Pour into a serving dish or mould. If using macaroons, line the dish or layer them as you prefer.
6. Chill until set. Serve cold with macaroons.
